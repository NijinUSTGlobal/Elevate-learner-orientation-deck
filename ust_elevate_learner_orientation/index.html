Evaluation, how it works

Your pod is reviewed together, and you are scored individually

Every sprint ends with a sprint review evaluation by HackerEarth panels. Your gate score is the weighted average of those evaluations.

Sprint endsYour pod submits its deliverable on SkillAssist
Pod reviewYour pod presents the deliverable together
Panel evaluatesHackerEarth panels of external experts run the viva and code review
Individual scoreYou are scored against pre-defined rubrics, not as a pod
Gate scoreWeighted average of all sprint evaluations
When

At the end of every sprint. Gate 1 is computed after Sprint 4 and Gate 2 at the end of your track.

Who

HackerEarth panels of external experts. Your trainers do not score you.

Against what

Pre-defined rubrics with clear scoring bands, the same for every learner. Your pod presents together, but each learner gets an individual score.

Pass criteria

70% weighted average clears the gate. Below 70%, one retest: an extra sprint with 75% or more in its evaluation.

Gate score calculator Move your sprint scores

Gate 1 score = (30% × Sprint 1) + (20% × Sprint 2) + (30% × Sprint 3) + (20% × Sprint 4)

Play Fair, what counts

Know what earns a card so you never get one

Sessions and attendanceCard path
  • Not on camera during a video call
  • Unexcused absence or late login beyond the allowed limit
  • Leaving a session early without approval
  • Skipping the daily scrum, reviews or pod ceremonies
  • Missing a Master Class without approval
Competency buildingCard path
  • Missed sprint deliverables or deadlines
  • Not completing mandatory eLearning or assignments on SkillAssist
  • Plagiarism or copied code
  • Undeclared AI use in assessments
  • Not contributing to your pod's deliverable
  • Skipping the daily update in SkillAssist
  • Missing your EOD status update to the Pod Coach
Generic conductCard path
  • Disrespectful or unprofessional behaviour
  • Ignoring communication from the programme team
  • Not following instructions from your Pod Coach, TPM or Master Trainer
  • Misusing chat or collaboration channels
  • Using unapproved tools or software for training work

Straight Red: LOI cancelled after due process

  • Impersonation or proxy attendance in any assessment
  • Cheating or malpractice in a review or viva
  • Harassment, discrimination or abusive conduct
  • Breach of UST data, security or IT policy
  • Leaking assessment or confidential content
  • Misrepresentation or fraudulent documents
This list is not complete

These are examples. Any act or omission that breaks the ways of working, the code of conduct or UST policy is Non-Adherence, whether or not it is listed here. The Manager, Operations and Governance decides how it is classified.

Allowed limits, such as late-login minutes, are published on SkillAssist.
Play Fair, due process

Before any Red Card, you always get to tell your side

A Red Card ends your LOI, so it goes through five recorded steps and two levels of review.

Logged with evidence, the same day.

2

You respond

Manager, Ops and Governance

You get 2 working days to give your side.

3

Recommendation

Manager, Ops and Governance

Reviews the evidence and your response.

4

Approval

Program Manager

A second level checks the decision.

5

Record closed

Program Lead

Outcome filed and archived.
